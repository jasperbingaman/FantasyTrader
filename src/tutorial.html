Draft Your Portfolio

Select 8 stocks from different sectors to build your portfolio. Just like fantasy football, each position matters!

Go head-to-head against friends in your league. Your portfolio's weekly returns determine who wins each matchup.

Complete courses, track your progress, and climb the leaderboards. Build real investing confidence through competition.
