Module 5: Tracking Performance & Next Steps

Learn how to track your progress and keep improving

Tracking Your Performance

Monitoring your portfolio's performance helps you understand what's working and what isn't. Look at your total returns, compare your results to the market, and review your trades regularly.

Tip

Don't get discouraged by short-term losses. Focus on learning and improving your strategy over time.

Setting Goals

Set clear, realistic goals for your trading. Are you aiming to grow your portfolio, learn new strategies, or compete in leagues? Goals help you stay focused and motivated.

Next Steps

  • Keep learning with intermediate and advanced modules
  • Join leagues and compete with others
  • Try new strategies and track your results
  • Stay updated with market news and events
