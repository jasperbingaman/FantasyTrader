Module 4: Portfolio Management & Diversification

Learn how to manage risk and build a strong portfolio

What is a Portfolio?

A portfolio is a collection of investments owned by an individual or organization. In FantasyTrader, your portfolio includes all the stocks you own.

Why Diversification Matters

Diversification means spreading your investments across different companies, industries, or asset types. This helps reduce risk because if one investment performs poorly, others may perform well.

Example

If you invest all your money in one stock and it drops, you lose a lot. If you invest in 10 different stocks, one drop won't hurt as much.

How to Diversify

  • Invest in companies from different industries (tech, healthcare, finance, etc.)
  • Mix large, stable companies with smaller, fast-growing ones
  • Consider different asset types (stocks, ETFs, etc.)

Monitoring Your Portfolio

Regularly check your portfolio's performance. Rebalance if one stock becomes too large a part of your holdings or if your goals change.
