Module 3: Buying and Selling Stocks

Learn the fundamentals of executing trades

How to Buy Stocks

Buying stocks is the process of purchasing shares of a company. Here's how it works:

1
Choose a Stock

Research and select a company you want to invest in based on your analysis.

2
Decide How Many Shares

Determine how many shares you want to buy based on your budget and strategy.

3
Place Your Order

Submit a buy order through your trading platform at the current market price.

4
Order Execution

Your order is matched with a seller and the trade is completed.

How to Sell Stocks

Selling stocks is how you realize profits or cut losses. The process is similar to buying:

1
Choose What to Sell

Select which stocks in your portfolio you want to sell.

2
Decide How Many Shares

Choose whether to sell all your shares or just a portion.

3
Place Your Order

Submit a sell order at the current market price.

4
Order Execution

Your order is matched with a buyer and the trade is completed.

Understanding Order Types

Market Order

Buy or sell immediately at the current market price. This is the most common type of order for beginners.

Limit Order

Set a specific price you're willing to pay (buy) or accept (sell). The order only executes if that price is reached.

Stop Loss Order

Automatically sell a stock if it falls to a certain price, helping to limit potential losses.

Example Trade

Buying Apple Stock

Current Price: $150 per share

Your Budget: $1,500

Shares You Can Buy: 10 shares ($1,500 ÷ $150)

Total Investment: $1,500

Note: In real trading, you'd also pay small fees called commissions.

Important Trading Concepts

Bid vs Ask

Bid: The highest price a buyer is willing to pay
Ask: The lowest price a seller is willing to accept
Spread: The difference between bid and ask prices

Market Hours

Stock markets are typically open Monday-Friday, 9:30 AM - 4:00 PM Eastern Time. Trades can only be executed during these hours.

Quick Quiz: What is a market order?

An order to buy or sell immediately at the current market price
An order that only executes at a specific price you set
An order to automatically sell if the price drops
An order that can only be placed during market hours
