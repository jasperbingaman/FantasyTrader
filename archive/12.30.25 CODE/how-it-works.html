How Fantasy Trader Works

Three simple steps to start building your investing skills through the thrill of competition.

1

Draft Your Team

Just like fantasy football, you'll draft a team — except your players are stocks. Each market sector maps to a football position, so you build a balanced portfolio naturally.

  • Pick 18 stocks from the top 1,000+ companies
  • Fill each position — one stock per sector minimum
  • Add 7 flex picks from any sector you believe in
  • Start 11 each week — bench the rest

2

Compete Weekly

Each week, you're matched against another player in your league. Your portfolio's return vs. theirs determines the winner. Win, and you capture 5% of their capital.

  • Head-to-head matchups — just like fantasy football
  • Weekly returns decide winners — not luck, skill
  • Winners take 5% from opponent's top holdings
  • Trade and adjust your lineup anytime during market hours
